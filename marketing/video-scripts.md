# SoundSauce Short-Form Video Scripts

Target platform: TikTok / Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts
Target audience: Beginner-intermediate producers using Vital (free synth)
App: soundsauce.app (free)
Recording: Desktop screen capture in dark mode, 1080p minimum

---

## Script 1: "Can AI reverse-engineer a synth sound?"

**Duration:** 40-45 seconds
**Format:** Screen recording with text overlays, no voiceover
**Background music vibe:** None needed — the 808 bass IS the audio. Silence between hits adds impact.

### Timing & Screen Direction

**[0-3s] HOOK**
- Text overlay: "Can AI figure out how this 808 was made?"
- Screen: Dark background, bold white text center screen
- Sound: Hard 808 bass hit plays (let it sustain and decay)
- CapCut: "Pop" text animation, slight screen shake on the bass hit

**[3-6s] SETUP**
- Text overlay: "I uploaded it to this free tool"
- Screen: Browser open to soundsauce.app/analyze, clean interface visible
- Action: Drag the 808 WAV file onto the upload zone
- CapCut: Speed ramp the drag-and-drop (1.5x)

**[6-12s] ANALYSIS**
- Text overlay: "It broke down everything"
- Screen: Waveform loads, click Analyze button, results appear
- Action: Pause briefly on key findings — waveform type "Sine", ADSR envelope visualization, filter settings
- CapCut: Speed the analysis wait to 2x, then normal speed when results appear. Subtle zoom (1.0 to 1.05x) on the results area

**[12-18s] RESULTS DETAIL**
- Text overlay: "Sine wave, pitch envelope, long decay — that's an 808"
- Screen: Show the Vital Preset tab, "Best Match" badge visible on the top preset
- Action: Briefly show tuning sliders, then click download
- CapCut: Add a small "ding" sound effect when the Best Match badge appears

**[18-23s] DOWNLOAD + VITAL**
- Text overlay: "Free Vital preset. One click."
- Screen: .vital file downloads, double-click to open Vital
- Action: Vital loads with the preset
- CapCut: Jump cut from download to Vital open (skip the file manager)

**[23-33s] PAYOFF — A/B Comparison**
- Text overlay: "Original" (left side)
- Screen: Play the original 808 sample (3 seconds)
- Text overlay: "AI Preset" (right side)
- Screen: Play the recreated preset in Vital (3 seconds)
- Text overlay: "Pretty close for 10 seconds of work"
- CapCut: Split-screen layout if possible, or quick back-to-back cuts

**[33-40s] CTA**
- Text overlay: "100% free. soundsauce.app"
- Screen: Show the homepage briefly
- Text overlay: "What sound should I upload next?"
- Sound: One final 808 hit
- CapCut: End card — pin a comment with "soundsauce.app — completely free, no credit card"

## Script 2: "How the [iconic sound] was actually made"

**Duration:** 50-55 seconds
**Format:** Screen recording with text overlays, no voiceover
**Background music vibe:** None — the sounds are the content. Silence between the demo clips creates contrast.
**Note:** Use a dubstep growl bass as the example. Do NOT use copyrighted recordings — record your own growl bass in Vital/Serum or use a royalty-free sample. This format is a repeatable series — swap the sound weekly.

### Timing & Screen Direction

**[0-4s] HOOK**
- Text overlay: "Ever wonder how a dubstep growl bass was made?"
- Screen: Dark background with text
- Sound: Play 1-2 seconds of the growl bass sample
- CapCut: Bass hit synced with text appearing, slight screen vibration

**[4-8s] SETUP**
- Text overlay: "Let's find out in 30 seconds"
- Screen: Open SoundSauce, drag in the growl bass sample
- Action: File uploads, waveform appears
- CapCut: Speed ramp upload to 2x

**[8-18s] ANALYSIS REVEAL — Zoom on key findings one by one**
- Text overlay: "The AI breaks it down..."
- Screen: Click Analyze, results appear
- Then zoom in on each finding:
  - Text overlay: "Sawtooth waveform" (zoom on waveform type card)
  - Text overlay: "Low-pass filter with heavy resonance" (zoom on filter data)
  - Text overlay: "LFO modulation = the wobble" (zoom on modulation section)
- CapCut: Each finding gets its own 2-3 second zoom shot. Quick cut between them. Add a subtle "reveal" sound on each.

**[18-28s] PRESET + RECIPE**
- Text overlay: "It tells you HOW to recreate it"
- Screen: Show the Vital Preset tab — "Wobble Bass" preset selected as best match
- Action: Click download
- Screen: Briefly flash the DAW Recipe tab — step-by-step instructions visible
- CapCut: 1.5x speed through the tab switching

**[28-40s] RECREATION — A/B**
- Text overlay: "Original"
- Screen: Play the original growl bass (3 seconds)
- Text overlay: "AI Preset"
- Screen: Play the Vital preset (3 seconds)
- Text overlay: "Not perfect — but I learned more in 2 minutes than a 20 min tutorial"
- CapCut: Split-screen or back-to-back cuts. The "not perfect" honesty builds trust.

**[40-50s] CTA**
- Text overlay: "Upload YOUR sounds — free at soundsauce.app"
- Screen: Homepage
- Text overlay: "What iconic sound should I try next? Comment below"
- CapCut: End card. Pin a comment asking for sound suggestions — this drives engagement and gives you next week's content.

### Series Notes
- This is a repeatable weekly format. Future episodes: "How the Daft Punk vocoder was made", "How the Flume future bass chord was made", "How the Metro Boomin 808 was made"
- Always record your own version of the reference sound to avoid copyright strikes
- The "not perfect but I learned something" angle is more authentic than claiming an exact match

## Script 3: "Stop buying presets. Make your own."

**Duration:** 38-42 seconds
**Format:** Fast-cut montage, screen recording with bold text overlays, no voiceover
**Background music vibe:** Energetic trap/electronic instrumental, very low volume (10%). The confidence of the edit carries the energy.

### Timing & Screen Direction

**[0-4s] HOOK — Slightly confrontational**
- Text overlay: "You don't need to spend $30 on preset packs"
- Screen: Quick flash of a preset marketplace page (blurred/generic, 1 second) then cut to black
- CapCut: "Cash register" or money-counting sound effect. Bold, oversized text.

**[4-8s] PIVOT**
- Text overlay: "Here's what to do instead"
- Screen: Cut to SoundSauce analyze page
- CapCut: Fast transition (swipe or zoom)

**[8-14s] STEP 1**
- Text overlay: "Find a sound you like"
- Screen: Quick 2-second clip of browsing Splice or hearing a sound in a song (keep it generic/blurred to avoid copyright)
- Text overlay: "Upload it"
- Screen: Drag file into SoundSauce, waveform appears
- CapCut: Jump cuts, 1.5x speed through the upload

**[14-22s] STEP 2**
- Text overlay: "Get a free preset in 10 seconds"
- Screen: Click Analyze, results appear (speed ramp the wait)
- Screen: Show Best Match preset, click download
- Screen: .vital file downloads
- CapCut: Speed ramp to 2x through analysis wait, normal speed when results land. Add a satisfying "pop" sound on download.

**[22-30s] STEP 3**
- Text overlay: "Customize it however you want"
- Screen: Open preset in Vital, briefly tweak a knob (filter cutoff or reverb)
- Screen: Play a few notes — sounds good
- CapCut: Let the audio breathe here — 3-4 seconds of the preset playing

**[30-38s] CTA**
- Text overlay: "SoundSauce. Free forever."
- Screen: Homepage
- Text overlay: "Save your money for plugins that actually matter"
- Text overlay: "Link in bio"
- CapCut: Slower pacing here for contrast with the fast first half. End on the provocative "save your money" line — it drives comments from people who agree and disagree.

### Notes
- This is a "hot take" style video — the slight controversy in "stop buying presets" drives engagement
- Keep the total runtime under 40 seconds — the fast pace is the point
- Consider adding a quick disclaimer at the end: "Presets are starting points — the real value is understanding the sound"
- The $30 figure is relatable and specific — more impactful than a vague "expensive"

## Script 4: "I built an AI that teaches you sound design"

**Duration:** 50-55 seconds
**Format:** Face cam (or voice-over) + screen recording mix
**Background music vibe:** Chill lo-fi beat, very quiet (10%). The personal story needs breathing room.
**Angle:** Builder/founder narrative. Humanizes the tool, builds emotional connection.

### Timing & Screen Direction

**[0-5s] HOOK — Personal and relatable**
- Text overlay: "I was tired of watching 20-minute YouTube tutorials to learn ONE sound"
- Screen: Face cam looking into camera, OR quick montage of a long YouTube tutorial with a hand clicking "skip" (relatable frustration)
- CapCut: Warm color grade on face cam. If no face cam, use white text on black with a slight typewriter animation.

**[5-10s] THE IDEA**
- Text overlay: "So I built something"
- Screen: Quick transition to SoundSauce homepage
- Voice-over or text: "What if AI could just tell you how a sound was made?"
- CapCut: Smooth zoom transition from face cam to screen recording

**[10-25s] MONTAGE — 3 sounds in rapid fire**

Upload a pad (5 seconds):
- Text overlay: "Upload a pad..."
- Screen: Drag file in, analysis runs, preset appears with Best Match badge, download
- CapCut: Speed ramp entire sequence to fit in 5 seconds. Brief audio clip of the pad sound.

Upload a lead (5 seconds):
- Text overlay: "Upload a lead..."
- Screen: Same flow — upload, analyze, download
- CapCut: Same speed ramp. Brief audio clip of a bright lead.

Upload a bass (5 seconds):
- Text overlay: "Upload a bass..."
- Screen: Same flow — upload, analyze, download
- CapCut: Same speed ramp. Brief audio clip of a deep bass.

**[25-35s] THE RESULT**
- Text overlay: "3 custom presets in under a minute"
- Screen: Show Vital with the 3 presets loaded
- Action: Play a quick chord/melody with each preset, 2-3 seconds each
- Text overlay: "No tutorials. No preset packs."
- CapCut: Let the audio breathe. Satisfying moment.

**[35-43s] COMMUNITY TEASE**
- Text overlay: "It's also a community"
- Screen: Quick flash of the Discover page with Sound Sauces visible
- Text overlay: "Share your breakdowns. Learn from others."
- Screen: Show a Sound Sauce detail page briefly (1-2 seconds)
- CapCut: Quick cuts, keep this section fast — it's a tease, not a demo

**[43-52s] CTA — Engagement-driven**
- Text overlay: "Free at soundsauce.app"
- Screen: Homepage or face cam
- Text overlay: "What sound should I try next?"
- Voice-over or text: "Seriously — comment a sound and I'll analyze it in the next video"
- CapCut: Pin a comment asking for sound suggestions. This drives comments AND gives you content ideas for the next video.

### Notes
- This is a one-time origin story — post it early in the content calendar for algorithm boost (personal stories perform well on TikTok)
- If you're comfortable on camera, face cam adds significant trust and personality
- If not comfortable on camera, voice-over with screen recording works fine — many successful tech TikToks use this format
- The "comment a sound" CTA is the most important part — it creates a content flywheel

## Script 5: "Beginner to sound designer in 60 seconds"

**Duration:** 55-60 seconds
**Format:** Tutorial-style screen recording with text overlays
**Background music vibe:** Upbeat, positive, slightly inspirational electronic. Keep it at 15% volume — this is an encouraging video, not an aggressive one.
**Angle:** Empowering beginners. "You can do this" energy.

### Timing & Screen Direction

**[0-4s] HOOK**
- Text overlay: "If you're a beginner producer, this will change everything"
- Screen: Bold text on dark background
- CapCut: Use a warm, inviting text animation (fade-in, not pop)

**[4-10s] THE PROBLEM — Relatable frustration**
- Text overlay: "Sound design feels impossible at first"
- Screen: Show Vital synth interface with LOTS of parameters visible — overwhelming
- Text overlay: "Too many knobs. No idea where to start."
- CapCut: Quick zoom out from Vital to emphasize the complexity. Maybe a slight blur effect to convey confusion.

**[10-18s] THE SOLUTION — Upload a sound**
- Text overlay: "But what if you could start from a sound you already like?"
- Screen: Open SoundSauce analyze page
- Text overlay: "Upload any sound..."
- Action: Drag in a pluck synth sample, waveform renders
- CapCut: Transition from "overwhelming Vital" to "clean SoundSauce UI" — the contrast should feel like relief

**[18-32s] THE BREAKDOWN — Show each insight**
- Text overlay: "SoundSauce tells you exactly how to recreate it"
- Screen: Click Analyze, results appear
- Zoom on results one by one (3 seconds each):
  - Text overlay: "Triangle waveform" (show waveform type card)
  - Text overlay: "Fast attack, short decay = pluck sound" (show ADSR visualization)
  - Text overlay: "F minor, 128 BPM" (show key/BPM cards)
- Screen: Show the Vital Preset tab, preset matched
- Text overlay: "And gives you a free preset that sounds like it"
- CapCut: Gentle zoom-ins (1.0 to 1.03x) on each result. Add subtle "reveal" sounds. Keep pacing relaxed — this audience needs time to absorb.

**[32-42s] THE COMPARISON**
- Text overlay: "Original sound"
- Screen: Play the uploaded pluck sample (3 seconds)
- Text overlay: "AI-generated preset"
- Screen: Play the Vital preset (3 seconds)
- Text overlay: "Pretty close, right?"
- CapCut: Split screen or back-to-back. Let both clips play fully — don't rush this moment.

**[42-52s] THE LEARNING MOMENT — This is the key differentiator**
- Text overlay: "Now open Vital and see what makes it work"
- Screen: Open Vital with the preset loaded
- Action: Point cursor at the oscillator section (triangle wave selected), then the ADSR (short decay visible), then the filter
- Text overlay: "Triangle wave. Short decay. That's what makes a pluck."
- Text overlay: "THAT'S how you learn sound design."
- CapCut: Slow, deliberate cursor movements. Each element gets 2-3 seconds. This is the emotional climax — the viewer realizes they can actually understand this.

**[52-60s] CTA**
- Text overlay: "Free tool. soundsauce.app"
- Screen: Homepage
- Text overlay: "What sounds do you want to learn? Comment below"
- CapCut: Warm fade-out. Pin a comment: "Drop a sound you want to learn and I'll analyze it"

### Notes
- This video should feel ENCOURAGING, not condescending — talk to beginners as equals who just need the right starting point
- The learning moment (42-52s) is what separates this from a product demo — position it as education, not advertising
- Pluck synth is a good example because it's simple (triangle wave + short decay) and the explanation is satisfying
- Consider adding captions for accessibility — many TikTok users watch without sound initially

## General Production Notes

### Recording Setup
- Browser at 1920x1080, zoom to 110-125% so UI elements read clearly on a phone screen
- Clean browser profile — no bookmarks bar, no extension icons visible
- Dark mode ON in SoundSauce — looks better on video and matches the producer aesthetic
- Have Vital synth open and ready before recording (pre-install if needed)
- Pre-load all audio files so uploads appear instant (no file picker browsing on screen)
- Close all notifications, other apps, taskbar clutter

### CapCut Editing Checklist
- Speed ramp all loading/waiting states to 2-4x
- Text overlays: white text, slight drop shadow, positioned in upper 2/3 (bottom is covered by TikTok UI)
- Keep each text overlay to 5-8 words maximum
- Use "Pop In" text animation sparingly (hook only), "Fade" for everything else
- Add subtle zoom (1.0 to 1.05x) on key moments for emphasis
- Background music at 10-15% volume maximum
- Export at 1080x1920 (9:16) for TikTok/Reels, or 1080x1080 for Instagram feed
- Add captions layer for accessibility
